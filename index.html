MellonPics Support

MellonPics is an instant camera that lives in your iPhone's Dynamic Island. Pull down from the top of the screen to open the camera, shoot, then close it and your prints slide out. Shake to develop them.

Common questions

How do I open the camera? Pull down from just under the pill at the top of the screen, or tap just under it. You can also add the MellonPics camera button to your Lock Screen or Control Center, ask Siri, or long-press the app icon.
Photos aren't developing. Give the phone a firm shake once a print has landed. Shaking long after it's done over-develops the shot, which is intentional — you can undo it from the print.
Camera permission denied. Go to Settings, then MellonPics, and turn on Camera.
Ran out of film. Pull the tab on the camera to rip open a new pack. Packs are free.
Deleted a print by accident. Deleted prints wait in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days.
Restore MellonPics Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase. Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. It also works with Family Sharing.
Something went wrong. If MellonPics crashed or froze, Settings → Diagnostics has the report iOS gave it. Tap Share and email it to us — it helps a lot.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

MellonPics has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server run by us. We never receive your photos, your notes, or your location. Everything below is about what stays on your iPhone and what goes into your own iCloud account, which Apple holds and we cannot read.

Camera. Used only to take the photo you're taking. Prints live in the app and, when you save or share one, wherever you send it.
Location. Used only to write the town a print was taken in on its back. Turning a coordinate into a place name goes through Apple's own services, not a MellonPics server, and only the town name is kept — never your exact position. Saved and shared photos carry the date they were taken but no location. Turn it off any time in MellonPics → Settings → Location.
iCloud sync. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, your prints and books sync between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That is your storage and your Apple Account — we have no access to it. Turning sync off stops it; nothing is deleted.
Group Baskets (not in the current App Store version; described here for early testers and a future release). This is the one part of the app that shares anything. A print you add to a group basket is uploaded to iCloud and can be seen and saved by everyone who has that basket's link, along with the town on its back and, if you set one, your name. Treat a basket's link or QR code like a key — anyone it's forwarded to can join. Prints you never add to a basket are never shared with anyone. If you report a print or a person, MellonPics opens an email to us with the basket, the print and that person's display name and iCloud user ID; it's only sent if you send it.
AI notes. Written with Apple Intelligence on your device. We never receive your photos or the notes.
Crash reports. If the app crashes or freezes, iOS hands it a technical report, which stays on your iPhone. It's only sent to us if you choose to share it from Settings → Diagnostics.
MellonPics Pro. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details and receive no personal information from a purchase. MellonPics Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so nothing renews and nothing is billed again. Pro is covered by Apple's standard licensed application terms.
Deleting things. A deleted print goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, then it's gone from your iPhone and from your iCloud sync. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your iPhone.
Children. MellonPics collects nothing, so there's nothing we could knowingly collect from a child.
