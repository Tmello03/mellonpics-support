MellonPics Support

MellonPics is an instant camera that lives in your iPhone's Dynamic Island. Swipe down to shoot, swipe up to print, then shake to develop.

Need help?

Email TrevorJMell3@proton.me and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

Photos aren't developing. Give the phone a firm, sustained shake right after a print ejects. Shaking too long on purpose over-develops the shot, which is intentional.
Camera permission denied. Go to Settings, then MellonPics, and enable Camera access.
Ran out of film. Rip open a new pack from the library to keep shooting.

Privacy Policy

MellonPics does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server run by us that your data ever passes through. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Camera. Used only to take the photo you're taking. Prints are saved straight to your iPhone's photo library and never leave your device.
Location. Used only to label where a print was taken. Turning a coordinate into a place name goes through Apple's own location services, not a MellonPics server — we never receive, see, or store your location.
AI captions. Captions are generated with Apple Intelligence, processed on your device (or Apple's Private Cloud Compute for the rare request that needs it, which Apple designs to be as private as on-device processing). We never receive your photos or captions.
